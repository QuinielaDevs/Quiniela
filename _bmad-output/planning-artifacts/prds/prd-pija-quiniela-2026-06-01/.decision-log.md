## [2026-06-01] Game Mechanics & Logistics Decisions
- **Decision 1: Sistema de Puntuación Estándar**
  - **Details:** 5 points for exact match score, 2 points for correct winner/draw but wrong score, 0 points for incorrect prediction.
  - **Rationale:** Aligns with standard competitive quiniela rules to balance predictability and ease of calculation.
- **Decision 2: Multiplicador Incremental por Pronóstico Anticipado**
  - **Details:** 
    - > 7 days in advance: 1.5x
    - 3 to 7 days in advance: 1.25x
    - 1 to 3 days in advance: 1.1x
    - < 24 hours in advance: 1.0x
    - Updating a prediction resets its multiplier to the current time threshold.
  - **Rationale:** Promotes deep strategic risk/reward for users who predict far in advance while remaining fair to last-minute adjustments.
- **Decision 3: Puntuación Decreciente de Premios Especiales**
  - **Details:** 
    - Predicted before kickoff of Match 1: 50 points.
    - Predicted during Group Stage (until R16 kickoff): 25 points.
    - Predicted during R16 and Quarterfinals: 10 points.
    - Predicted after Quarterfinals: 0 points (locked).
  - **Rationale:** Prevents late-stage predictions from carrying equal weight to riskier initial predictions while keeping the awards feature active longer.
- **Decision 4: Gestión de Pagos y Bajas de Miembros**
  - **Details:** Use a public visual status flag ("Pagado" vs "Pendiente") next to names in the leaderboard for social pressure. Do not freeze points or block logins. Grant the Administrator the authority to permanently remove ("dar de baja") members from the league if they do not pay or disrupt the league.
  - **Rationale:** Keeps game engagement high for all users without creating technical barriers or unnecessary blocking features, while giving administrators complete group logistics control.
- **Decision 5: Pila Tecnológica y Sincronización API**
  - **Details:** Next.js + Supabase + Vercel stack. Data synced via API-Football using a Pull-and-Cache model (running a cron every 30 minutes only during active match hours). Client-side recalculation of standings from real scores to stay under Supabase Free Tier connection limits (200 WebSockets).
  - **Rationale:** Maximizes scalability and development speed under a true zero-cost infrastructure plan.
  - **UPDATE (2026-06-04 — Epic 7):** La parte de **sincronización vía API-Football queda SUPERSEDED**: el plan Free no da acceso a `season=2026`. Se reemplaza por seed (datos reales en `supabase/seed-data/worldcup-2026/`) + captura de resultados por el admin + motor automático de avance de fase. El stack (Next.js/Supabase/Vercel) y la recalculación client-side se conservan. Ver `sprint-change-proposal-2026-06-04.md`.

## [2026-06-01] PRD Draft Feedback & Clarifications
- **Decision 6: Exclusividad de Onboarding con Google OAuth**
  - **Details:** User onboarding will be exclusively handled via Google OAuth. Registration via traditional email/password credentials is removed.
  - **Rationale:** Minimizes registration friction, eliminates the need for email confirmation workflows, and guarantees secure single-tap access on mobile devices.
- **Decision 7: Carga Manual de Favoritos para Premios Especiales**
  - **Details:** The list of candidates/favorites for the Tournament Awards (Champion, Top Scorer, MVP) will be loaded manually into the database by the platform administrator.
  - **Rationale:** Eliminates the need for real-time third-party player-search API integrations, saving resources and ensuring clean database mapping.
- **Decision 8: Gestión de Partidos Suspendidos o Pospuestos**
  - **Details:** If a match is officially canceled or postponed outside the tournament timeframe, predictions for it yield 0 points. Any pending challenges for that match are immediately canceled, and points locked in escrow are fully refunded to the participants' available balance.
  - **Rationale:** Restores fairness to all players by nullifying predictions and returning staked points if a match cannot be played as scheduled.

## [2026-06-01] Post-Finalization Revisions (Discussion & Agreement)
- **Decision 9: Módulo de Desafíos Unificado (Desafíos Directos y Abiertos)**
  - **Details:** Unified head-to-head (1v1) and group challenges under a single database table (`public.challenges` + `public.challenge_participants`). Direct challenges require oponent acceptance; Open challenges act as multi-player pools open to all league members. Points are held in escrow and distributed to the winner(s) upon match completion.
  - **Rationale:** Simplifies database structure while delivering both personal rivalry and collective engagement features.
- **Decision 10: Multiplicadores Ajustados por Partido**
  - **Details:** Calculations are based per individual match kickoff. Set week-based thresholds starting at 1.2x and incrementing by 0.2: > 5 weeks (2.0x), 4 to 5 weeks (1.8x), 3 to 4 weeks (1.6x), 2 to 3 weeks (1.4x), 1 to 2 weeks (1.2x), < 1 week (1.0x). Edits are allowed until 1 minute before kickoff but update the timestamp and recalculate the multiplier.
  - **Rationale:** Rewards long-term forecasting risk with a clean, progressive scale while keeping score calculation simple and preventing early-stage edits from holding unfair multipliers.
- **Decision 11: Formulario de Configuración del Administrador**
  - **Details:** Added settings during league creation for the Admin to set "Requires Payment", "Amount", and "Payment Instructions". If payment is enabled, it triggers the visual payment tracking labels on leaderboards and displays payment instructions on invitation screens.
  - **Rationale:** Simplifies group payment collection offline while ensuring transparency.
- **Decision 12: Compartido Viral (WhatsApp Banter & OpenGraph Cards)**
  - **Details:** Integrated WhatsApp sharing with pre-formatted competitive messages and smart invitation links (/desafio/xyz). Uses dynamic OpenGraph tags to show banner previews in WhatsApp, taking users to a direct action landing page for joining the challenge via Google OAuth in 1-click.
  - **Rationale:** Drives engagement outside the app using WhatsApp as the natural conversation channel.

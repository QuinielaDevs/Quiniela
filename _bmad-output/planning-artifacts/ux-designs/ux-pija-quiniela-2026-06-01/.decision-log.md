# UX Decision Log: Quiniela Mundial FIFA 2026

This log serves as the canonical memory and audit trail for all UX and design decisions.

## [2026-06-01] Initialization
- **Decision:** Initialized the BMad UX workspace `ux-pija-quiniela-2026-06-01`.
- **Rationale:** Start of the UX design phase based on the finalized PRD and brainstorming session.

## [2026-06-01] Discovery Decisions (PRD Focus)
- **Decision 1: Visual Identity and Style (Stadium Lights)**
  - **Theme:** Default dark-mode with deep carbon surfaces, off-white text, vivid emerald green accents (primary actions and auto-save success indicators), and trophy gold highlights (points, multipliers, and badges).
  - **UI System:** Inherits from Tailwind CSS and Shadcn UI.
  - **Typography:** Uses Google Fonts' "Outfit" for headings/titles and "Inter" for UI and data elements.
- **Decision 2: Information Architecture**
  - **Surface 1: Onboarding Welcome Screen.** Contextual entry point for smart deep-links showing creator invitation, payment requirements (if active), Zelle/cash instructions, and a single "Google Sign-In" OAuth button.
  - **Surface 2: Dashboard (Today's Matches).** Main prediction area with responsive mobile layout, large tactical target buttons (`+`/`-`) to modify goals, debounced auto-save status indicator, and kickoff locking.
  - **Surface 3: Leaderboard (Standings).** Cumulative points table showing user avatars, points, visual indicators of unpaid members (if payment is required), and a sub-tab to toggle between general table and round/jornada standings.
  - **Surface 4: Live Leaderboard Projection.** WebSocket-driven view active during games, demonstrating current live scores and animated toasts/notifications when gol events alter user standings ("Impacto de Gol").
  - **Surface 5: Duelos (Challenges Panel).** Unified listing of Direct 1v1 and Open group challenges, with escrow balance display, pending invites, active wagers, and a share-to-WhatsApp trigger.
  - **Surface 6: Tournament Awards.** Sub-pane or modal to predict Champion, Top Scorer, and MVP, referencing pre-loaded favorite candidates with visual indications of decreasing points reward depending on the current tournament phase.
- **Decision 3: Core UX Interactions & Motion**
  - **Debounce Feedback:** A subtle 500ms delay after clicking `+`/`-` triggers the Server Action write; success displays a quick green glow around the score card followed by a permanent checkmark `✓` icon.
  - **Banter Text & Sharing:** WhatsApp invitations auto-generate competitive text ("¡Oye @Diego, te reto a que Argentina le gana a Francia por 5 puntos de la quiniela! Entra y acepta aquí: /desafio/xyz") and load enriched OpenGraph previews.
- **Decision 4: Payment Banner & Logistics**
  - **Visual Indicator:** If "Requires Payment" is enabled in league settings, a red flag "Pendiente de Pago" is displayed on the leaderboard next to unpaid users. A central sticky banner at the top of their dashboard reminds them of the payment details (Zelle/cash instructions) but does *not* restrict predictions to preserve high retention.
- **Decision 5: Accessibility Floor**
  - **Contrast & Sizes:** High contrast text-on-dark. Interactive tap targets for `+`/`-` buttons are at least 48x48px on mobile to prevent misclicks.

## [2026-06-01] Coaching Path - Visual Choice
- **Decision:** Selected Theme 3 (Championship Gold) with customizations.
  - **Customization:** Integrated Turf Green (`#10B981`) for affirmative success statuses (e.g. "Guardado ✓" indicator and paid/verified status flags). Main layout keeps deep indigo background (`#0D1B2A`), navy cards (`#1B263B`), crimson functional primary colors (`#E63946`), and trophy gold accents (`#E9C46A`) for scores and points.
  - **Rationale:** Direct selection by the user to combine World Cup prestige colors with clear athletic green success signals.
